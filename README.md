# HAPZO Local ERP + DPP

HAPZO Local runs on Windows with a browser interface, a Node.js server, and a SQLite database. It follows the [ERP Prompt conversation](https://chatgpt.com/share/6aff264c-0c04-83e8-bbb8-e42757787a3d) and the [HAPZO operating model](HAPZO_ERP_Operating_Model.md).

## Start the application

1. Double click **Start HAPZO.cmd**. Node.js 24 or newer is required. The launcher installs locked dependencies once if needed, starts the server, and opens [HAPZO Local](http://127.0.0.1:4380/). To launch from a terminal, run `npm start` in this folder.
2. On first launch, create your company and administrator login. The app starts with an empty company.
3. In **Settings & data**, select **Open a separate sample company** to try the workflows. Use the company selector to return to the empty workspace.

Records and document originals are stored in **data/hapzo.sqlite**. You can close the browser without losing posted records. Press Ctrl+C in the launcher window to stop a server started there.

## Day-to-day workflow

| Area | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Company masters | Enter buyers, suppliers, processors, factory locations, materials, machines and custom routes. Local logins can work across companies according to their memberships. |
| Buyer orders | Save the original PO and its revisions, add style and size lines, and approve the specification, BOM, costing and delivery schedule. Revisions retain their approved snapshots. |
| Fabric specifications | Use **Fabric specs** for quick calculations or save the inputs inside a buyer PO line. Woven specifications calculate EPI and PPI, and knitted specifications calculate WPI, CPI, stitch density for plain single jersey, and machine gauge from dimensions. Both estimate GSM and yarn counts from known construction and calculate limits from buyer tolerances. |
| Purchase and stores | Compare BOM demand, reservations and released stock. Record quotes and requisitions, issue supplier POs, receive lots by stock unit, and record intake weight and length. |
| Production | Release work orders and post exact input IDs, output IDs, waste, rejects and measured conversions. Use the four woven and knitted dyeing routes or a custom route. A work order can post multiple output lots across runs. |
| Quality and standards | Record lab reports and release, quarantine, rework or reject lots. Review standard evidence separately. Upload certificates or annexes to extract text and suggested fields, then record the issuer or lookup confirmation. |
| Warehouse and job work | Transfer or split lots with genealogy. Send outside jobs by challan and reconcile processed output, unused returns and loss against the outstanding quantity. |
| Dispatch and finance | Dispatch whole cartons or other released goods, record transport legs and ports, print packing lists and issue invoices. Post landed cost, entered GST components, inventory relief, vouchers and settlements. Reports include the trial balance, ledger, profit and loss, balance sheet, open balances and GST entries. |
| Trace and passport | Print lot QR labels and scan using a camera, image file or keyboard scanner. Build a passport from a finished lot. Assess all 49 project fields, verify applicable values, then review and publish. The stable link serves fields approved for public access. |
| Equipment and resources | Record machine maintenance and enter meter values through the reading form or the authenticated transaction endpoint. Verified readings can be attributed to the same machine and production date. |

## Yarn dyeing and component routes

- **Yarn dyeing → Setup** approves a fabric route per BOM or tech pack component: A (yarn dyeing → knitting → finishing), B (yarn dyeing → warp and weft preparation → weaving → finishing), C (knitting → fabric dyeing → finishing) or D (warp and weft preparation → weaving → fabric dyeing → finishing). Sizing, fabric washing and fabric printing join the route only when selected. A new approval supersedes the previous one; released work orders keep the route, BOM revision and PO line revision they were released with. Work orders without a component route behave as before.
- Setup also approves dye recipes (shade, dose per kg, liquor ratio, planned water, steam, electricity and yield) with an internal cost rate card.
- **Work order** releases the yarn dyeing job and opens dye batches on a machine and recipe. **Material issue** moves yarn into batch work in progress. Lots stocked in cones need a kg basis measured at intake or weighed at issue. **Process entry** records each step with start and end time, machine, operator, shift, temperature, utilities and chemical lots. **Output and return** posts partial outputs (accepted or rework, in kg or cones with measured kg), waste, rejects and measured gain or loss, returns unused yarn to its lot, closes the batch and reverses the latest posting with a reason. **Lot history** shows owner, custodian and location separately, direct inputs, backward and forward trace, and every posting.
- Output consumption is allocated across the issued lots by the share of work in progress unless the actual lot consumption is entered. Chemicals and utilities are absorbed into each output by the same share (method `HZ-YD-ALLOC-1`). The DPP activity rows carry electricity, water and steam with that method version. Internal labour, machine and utility absorption is posted as GST-free cost on the output lot; supplier and job-work charges with GST still use **Invoices & costs**.
- The Standard Lock runs on issue, chemical consumption and output for the work order’s schemes. Pending, expired or withdrawn evidence and lots on Standard Hold block the posting regardless of quality status. Route skips, over-issues, duplicate references and links to lots outside the batch are rejected.
- The sample company includes batch `DYB-26031-01` on PO `DC-26031`. Look up `YRN-C-02` in **Scan & trace** to follow it into `DY-RED-01`, `DY-RED-02` and warp beam `BEAM-YD-01`.
- Run `npm test` to check the yarn dyeing, ginning, fibre preparation and spinning postings, Standard Lock, route checks, reversal and the SQLite posting path.

## Fabric dyeing

- **Fabric dyeing → Setup** approves dye recipes for knitted, woven or any fabric. Each records colour and shade, a pretreatment for the route (none, scouring, scouring and bleaching, desizing with scouring and bleaching, or mercerising), dyes and chemicals per kg, liquor ratio, utilities and rates. The recipe fixes the required steps in order: loading, pretreatment (if any), dyeing, washing and unloading; a re-dye batch uses re-dye, washing and unloading. Revisions supersede earlier recipes for new batches.
- **Roll inward** receives greige roll by roll, as own material or customer material for a named job. It records kg, metres, the customer's roll number, construction, GSM, width, and whether the fabric was woven from a sized warp.
- **Dye batch** combines rolls on one machine for one recipe. A commission batch takes only that customer's rolls for that job. Each roll is checked against the recipe's fabric kind. A woven roll from a sized warp that has not been desized (including mill-woven rolls traced to a sized beam) needs a recipe whose pretreatment includes desizing. Steps record machine, shift, operator, dye and chemical lots, temperature, liquor ratio, water, steam, electricity and verified meter readings.
- **Unload rolls** posts a receipt of dyed rolls: roll ID, dry-equivalent kg, wet kg, metres, the source greige rolls and an optional leftover flag. Stock is held in dry-equivalent kg (conditioned weighing, moisture meter or standard regain); the wet weight is kept as a separate measurement. Each dyed roll draws from its own source rolls (roll-to-roll genealogy) with its share of waste. Shade-off rolls can be posted for rework and re-dyed. Unloaded greige ends return to their rolls.
- **Commission and outward** shows each customer job's balance: greige received against consumption, held and returned greige, dyed output against waste, and delivered and held dyed rolls. It also posts delivery challans to the owner and outward issues to an outside dye house. A claimed outward issue is blocked when the dye house's verified scope does not cover fabric dyeing at that facility.
- In the sample company, customer rolls `CG-001`, `CG-002` and `CG-003` (job `CJO-F-001`) are dyed together in batch `FDB-CUS-01` and unloaded in two receipts: `CD-001` and `CD-002`, then `CD-003` and leftover `CD-003L`. They return on challans `DC-F-001` and `DC-F-002`, and the job balance reconciles to zero. Issuing own GOTS roll `OG-001` to Example Dye House for fabric dyeing is blocked, because its verified scope `GOTS-DYEHOUSE-01` covers yarn dyeing only.

## Fabric finishing

- In **Manufacturing → Fabric finishing**, approve an ordered sequence against an approved PO line or fabric component route. Enter one operation per row and use `|` to add its approved settings. Sequences can include drying, stentering, compacting, calendering, heat setting, sanforising and other named finishes.
- Release a work order against the plan and issue the actual roll lots in kg. Post the operations in sequence with their machines, times and any chemical lots used. Each batch keeps its plan and approved fabric specification revision.
- Post each finished roll with its source lot and input kg, finished net kg, metres, width in cm and measured GSM. Use **Remnant** for a separate piece of finished stock. Record an unused remnant return to the source roll, including measured length when available.
- Release the output in **Quality & lab**. Released rolls can be dispatched on a finished fabric sales PO or issued to cutting on a garment PO, subject to stock allocation and the approved route.
- Scan the finished roll QR or enter its lot ID in **Scan & trace** to see actual finishing operations, measurements, the selected production route, and upstream dye batches and yarn lots.

## Washing

- Put **Yarn washing**, **Fabric washing**, **Cut bundle washing**, or **Garment washing** on the approved PO route. A fabric component route can also include **Fabric washing** before finishing. Release a work order for that operation in **Manufacturing → Work orders**. Use the same stock unit as the primary lots.
- In **Manufacturing → Washing**, approve a recipe revision and load an in-house machine, or send a commission batch on an outward challan. Enter one input lot per line with its stock quantity. Add a measured kg value and weighing slip when the issue has a separate weighing.
- Record the actual cycle, machine load, utilities and chemical lots. In-house chemicals are issued from stock. Commission chemical lots are recorded from the processor's report.
- Post each actual receipt against its original input lot. Enter usable, re-wash, rejected and loss quantities, along with measured output kg and any before/after readings. For a return with several input lots, post one line at a time using the same inward challan. Remaining kg represents the weight issued against the outstanding stock quantity. For a re-wash, release a rework work order for the same PO line and washing step and issue the re-wash lot.
- On a commission batch, use **Add charge** to post an approved **Job work** cost against the returned lot. The charge carries the processor, batch, outward challan and actual receipt. Post it to the supplier's payable ledger in **Accounts & vouchers** when approved for accounting.

## Printing

- Use **Fabric printing** on a fabric route, **Printing** after cutting for panels, or **Garment printing** after sewing. In **Manufacturing → Printing**, approve the artwork revision against the PO line or fabric component route. Record the design, method, placement, approval and required curing or washing sequence.
- Release a work order against that artwork and issue its actual roll, bundle or garment lot IDs in their stock unit. For commission printing, select the processor, enter the exact facility and post an outward challan. Buyer site and chemical requirements use **Standards & evidence**.
- Record the quantity printed from each input lot, the actual machine, ink or paste batch and chemical lots. Post required curing or washing before receiving the stock. Separate partial receipts can draw against the same run without consuming the remaining processor balance.
- Enter one line per accepted, misprint or rejected lot. Record waste in the issued stock unit. Misprints can be issued on a rework order against the same artwork revision. Released accepted stock can be issued to the next approved operation, with the related work order and output shown on the print batch.

## Weaving

- **Weaving → Setup** records looms (type, reed width, weft selectors, shedding) and approves weave constructions: weave, total ends, reed, reed space, picks per cm, warp and weft counts, crimp, the warp colour sequence (colour, ends) and weft insertion sequence (colour, picks) for yarn-dyed fabric, whether the warp is sized and its size recipe, the weft preparation required, and the loom type. A sized construction needs a route with Sizing and Desizing; route approval now rejects Sizing without Desizing.
- **Warp path**: allocate warp yarn to a warping order (Yarn dyeing → Knitting, warp and weft), then open a warp set. Each issued lot must carry that warp allocation and match the colour and recorded count. Doff warper's beams with ends, metres and kg; for an unsized warp the beam must carry all ends and is the loom beam. A sizing set creels the warper's beams and records the size chemical lots used; the loom beam posts only when beams carrying all ends and a size chemical lot are linked. **Drawing-in or tying-in** dresses a loom beam on one loom; tying-in names the beam it knots onto.
- **Weft path**: allocate weft lots to the weaving order, record preparation (rewinding, pirn winding, conditioning or waxing). When the construction requires preparation, an unprepared lot cannot be issued to the loom.
- **Loom**: open a run on a loom matching the construction's type, width and colour count. Gait the dressed beam and issue weft. Record the feeder plan (feeder, weft lot) on the first weaving step, and each **weft package change** at its loom metre. A change cannot be dated into rolls already posted. **Doff a roll** with metres and measured kg: warp is drawn from the beam by length with crimp, weft is the roll weight less warp, and the roll is cut into segments at every weft change. Each segment keeps its feeder and weft lots, and the roll's genealogy carries every lot with its kg. Posting is blocked when the run has no dressed beam, the beam does not trace to warp yarn, no feeder plan is recorded, or a feeder lot lacks the weft the roll needs. Return the beam remnant and unused weft, mark short pieces as remnants, and record loom waste.
- **Roll trace** shows a roll's loom metres, beam and dressing, warp yarn and size chemical lots, the selected route, the weft by segment, and where the roll went.
- In the sample company, greige construction `GW-PL-40` (route D with sizing) goes from `YRN-WP-01` to warper's beams `WB-061-1..4`, then sizing with `CHEM-SZ-01` to loom beam `LB-061-1`, drawn in on `LOOM-R1`. Loom run `LR-26061` switches feeder F1 from weft lot `YRN-WF-01` to `YRN-WF-02` at 450 m: roll `GR-061-02` has segments 300–450 m and 450–600 m with their own weft lots. That roll is desized (`DS-061-02`) and dyed (`FD-061-02`). Yarn-dyed check `YD-CHK-01` (route B) weaves a red/navy warp and two-colour rewound weft into `YC-062-01`, finished as `FF-YC-062-01`.

## Knitting

- **Knitting → Setup** records each knitting machine's type, gauge, diameter and feeders as an effective-dated profile. It also approves fabric constructions per component (body, rib, collar, cuff, placket or other): structure, yarn feed (role, count, count system and colour), colour sequence in courses per repeat, machine type and gauge, stitch length, GSM, width and planned yield. A revision supersedes the earlier construction for new runs.
- **Production orders** are released separately for body, rib and other components, each on its approved component route. Greige knitting from undyed yarn uses route C (knitting → fabric dyeing → finishing). Yarn-dyed knitting from dyed cones uses route A (yarn dyeing → knitting → finishing). A run opens only when the machine profile matches the construction's type and gauge, and the construction matches the route.
- **Cone issue and return** issues cones or kg by lot. Each lot is checked for material (dyed or undyed), colour against the colour sequence, count against the feed when the lot carries a recorded count (spun yarn does), standard claims and yarn allocations. Unused cones return to their lots.
- **Machine and shift** records each step with machine, knitter, shift, times and energy. **Rolls** doffs one or more rolls per posting, each with its roll ID, kg and metres where measured, plus knitting loss and rejects. Every colour in the sequence must be issued first, and each roll links to the yarn lots consumed by its share of the weight.
- **Roll trace** shows a roll's construction, machine, gauge, run and shift, its actual yarn lots (colour, count, cones and kg in the roll, and the dye batch or spin lot behind each), the selected route with each operation's status, and where the roll went.
- In the sample company, striped body roll `KR-STR-001` (red `DYR-01` and navy `DYN-01`, route A) is finished into `FF-STR-001`, and greige rib roll `KR-RIB-001` (30s Ne `YRN-30C-B`, route C) is dyed into `FD-RIB-001`.

## Yarn dyeing: own material and commission work

- **Commission and custody** receives undyed yarn as own material (from a supplier) or as customer-owned yarn for commission dyeing. A batch opened for a commission customer accepts only that customer's yarn, and its dyed lots stay in the customer's ownership while this mill is the custodian. An own-material batch rejects customer yarn. **Deliver commission-dyed yarn** returns released lots to their owner on a delivery challan. The same tab lists customer yarn on hand, commission batches, deliveries, and yarn with outside dye houses (processor custody).
- Recipes record colour and shade, the specified pretreatment and its bath conditions, and whether yarn is rewound. These fix the required steps in order: package winding, pretreatment (if specified), dyeing, soaping or washing, drying and rewinding. A re-dye batch for shade-off yarn uses re-dye, washing, drying and rewinding. Output is blocked until the required steps are recorded. Loading, shade correction, fixing, hydro-extraction and unloading can be recorded at any point after the first required step. Recipes approved before step control are not enforced.
- **Recipe revision**: approve a new revision that names the one it revises and a reason. The earlier revision is superseded for new batches; open batches keep their pinned revision.
- **Knitting, warp and weft** allocates a yarn lot to a knitting, warping or weaving work order for one use. Production then lets that work order use the yarn only for that use, and other work orders only the unallocated balance. Batch issues to dyeing, ginning, blending or spinning respect the same allocations.
- When a claimed standard is blocked because a verified certificate for the site does not cover the operation, the Standard Lock message names that certificate and the operations it covers.
- In the sample company, undyed lot `YRN-UD-01` splits into red batch `DYB-RED-01` (two dyed lots, `DYR-01` and `DYR-02`) and navy batch `DYB-NAVY-01` (`DYN-01`, plus shade-off `DYN-RW`, re-dyed on revised recipe `NAVY-R1` r2 into `DYN-02`). `DYR-02` cones go to knitting (`KNT-YD-01`). `DYR-01` is allocated as warp and `DYN-01` as weft for weaving (`BEAM-YD-02` → `GRG-YD-01`). Commission batch `DYB-CUS-01` dyes `Example Knit Brand` yarn and delivers it on `DC-CUS-001`. Issuing `YRN-CUS-GOTS` to the GOTS commission batch `DYB-CUS-GOTS` is blocked, because verified scope `GOTS-MILL-01` covers knitting and weaving but not yarn dyeing.

## Ginning and fibre preparation

- **Ginning & fibre → Setup** sets the mill's tracking: lint as **Each bale** (one lot per bale) or **Bale lot** (one lot carrying its bale list), and the handoff to spinning as **Blend lot**, **Shift lot** or **Each can / lap**. It also approves gin plans (lint, seed and waste outturn) and blend plans (component shares by variety, origin, lot mark or item code, with a tolerance).
- **Seed cotton inward** records the grower or farmer group, farm ID, origin, variety, weighbridge gross and tare, moisture, trash and ticket. Stock is the net weight. **Lint bale inward** is a separate entry point for purchased bales: supplier, ginner, origin, lot mark, variety, and each bale's net and gross weight with optional HVI values.
- **Gin run** issues seed cotton, records each step and presses lint bales with seed and gin waste in one posting. Consumed seed cotton must equal lint + seed + waste, with any measured moisture change shown separately. Lint and seed share the seed cotton inputs, utilities and absorbed cost by mass (method `HZ-GIN-MASS-1`).
- **Blend and laydown** issues actual bale weights to a blend, then records bale selection, laydown (with the bale lots laid), opening, cleaning, blending and measured droppings. The handoff posts prepared fibre for spinning at the configured granularity. It is blocked while the issued mix is outside the approved blend tolerance.
- **Bale and blend scan** accepts a keyboard-scanner payload (`HZ:LOT:…`, a bale number or a blend number). It shows supplier and origin, the evidence used and its current status, standard eligibility, physical availability and blend-plan fit. A scan never moves stock. **Add to the bale issue draft** fills the issue form for review, and nothing posts until you submit it.
- In the sample company, blend `BL-26-01` takes 510 kg from ginned lot `LINT-B` and 340 kg from purchased lot `LINT-A`. Scan `BL-26-01`, or open `PF-BL-26-01` in Lot history, to trace back to both lots and to seed cotton `SC-26-001`. Bale lot `LINT-C` claims OCS without verified evidence and scans as ineligible.

## Spinning

- **Spinning → Setup** enables ring spinning, rotor spinning or both for the mill. It also approves count plans with count and count system (Ne, Nm, tex or den), tolerance, composition, twist, cone weight, planned yield, noil and waste, and a route: carded or combed, ring or rotor.
- Each route fixes the process steps. Ring routes run blow room, carding, breaker drawing, roving, ring spinning and winding. Combed routes add lap forming (comber preparation) and combing before finisher drawing. Rotor routes have no roving or winding. Steps must be recorded in order, with machine, shift, operator, times, production and waste kg, hank and deliveries.
- **Work order** releases spinning against a yarn buyer order line or for stock, then opens a spin lot on a count plan. **Fibre issue** accepts a prepared fibre lot from **Ginning & fibre**; its blend, bale weights and blow room steps are pulled forward, and blow room counts as done. Lint issued directly must go through blow room on the spin lot.
- **Yarn packing** posts a yarn lot after every route step is recorded. It captures good yarn kg, cones with their first and last IDs (or a list), cartons, the measured count, CV and test report, comber noil as its own lot, waste and rejects. Yarn outside the count tolerance goes on quality hold. Yarn is allocated to the work order's yarn line unless you allocate it differently.
- **Yarn sale and transfer** lists yarn lots and their movements. Sell through **Dispatch & logistics** and invoice as usual. Transfer to another store, or issue yarn to yarn dyeing, knitting or warping.
- **Yarn scan** retrieves a yarn lot's input bale history: each bale lot and its bale numbers, origin, supplier, ginner or gin run, the weight issued to the blend, and the share attributable to this yarn lot.
- In the sample company, blend `BL-26-01` is spun on spin lot `SPL-26-01` into `YRN-30C-A` (330 kg yarn, 70 kg noil, 25 kg waste) and `YRN-30C-B` (310 kg yarn, 60 kg noil, 21 kg waste). Lot A is shipped on `SHP-26040-01`; lot B moves to the weaving preparatory store. Scan `YRN-30C-A` to see bale lots `LINT-B` and `LINT-A`.

## Backups and local operation

- Double click **Backup HAPZO.cmd** or run `npm run backup`. SQLite writes a dated copy under `data/backups` while the system is running. The copy includes documents, access assignments and company records.
- To restore to a separate instance, stop that instance. Put the backup in a new directory as `hapzo.sqlite` and set `HAPZO_DATA_DIR` to that directory before starting HAPZO. Keep the previous data directory available to switch back.
- **Settings & data → Export company records** downloads the current company’s JSON for review or reporting. Use the SQLite backup for a restorable copy.
- The server listens on `127.0.0.1`. Change port `4380` with `HAPZO_PORT`. A published passport resolves on the PC by default. For authorised phones or computers on the local network, configure an HTTPS reverse proxy and set `HAPZO_PUBLIC_URL` to its origin before printing labels.
- A Buyer login sees its own approved lines, shipments, invoices and published links. Staff roles apply at the company level.
- The earlier `HAPZO_Decaste_ERP_Pilot.html` can still be opened to access or export its browser records. That data is stored separately from the SQLite application.

## Buyer claim checks

- In **Standards & evidence**, upload the certificate and any annexes together to read their pages. Review the suggested values and flags, check the official lookup or contact the issuer, and record how the evidence was verified.
- Use **Require evidence for a buyer PO or site** to set the actual lock point, coverage type, accepted evidence and rule revision. Apply requirements to a PO, PO line or named facility. A chemical requirement with a blank code checks each chemical recorded at that operation.
- Use **Release a product or label claim** to check the exact SKU or component. For MADE IN GREEN, record the verified product certification, supply-chain confirmation, covered STeP sites and Product ID. The release register records the evidence and references for that lot.
- When a chain-of-custody rule requires transaction evidence, enter the seller, buyer, shipment reference, material code and eligible quantity from the source. The receipt and dispatch forms accept the shipment reference shown on the evidence.

## Maintaining the books

- In **Ledgers**, create or maintain accounts by group. Use **Post opening balances** for a balanced bank, asset, liability or equity opening voucher. Carry in unpaid customer and vendor documents with **Post opening party bill** so each bill can be settled by reference.
- In **Accounts & vouchers**, post the source invoice or bill and select its party. When recording a receipt or payment, enter an amount against each bill covered by that voucher. Corrections post as dated reversals with a reason.
- Use **Day book** to filter and open vouchers, and **Ledgers** to see opening, running and closing balances and pending bills. **Financial reports** shows the trial balance, period profit and loss, balance sheet and outstanding bills as of the selected date. Voucher lines and ledger statements export as CSV.

## Garment tech packs

- In **Buyer orders → Create buyer PO**, select the original PDF, PNG or JPEG to fill the **Buyer PO number** field. HAPZO shows the page and reader used and leaves the field editable. **Import buyer PO with AI** checks the original PO the same way before showing the draft. Excel workbooks use the AI provider to read the number from cells.
- With a Gemini key configured in `.env`, selecting the original PO under **Create buyer PO** sends it to Gemini to identify the company placing the order. HAPZO selects a unique match from Company masters. For a new buyer, review the name and currency and click **Save buyer** to add and select the buyer before saving the PO.
- Set the reader in **Settings & data → Buyer PO number recognition**. **Local SoMark OXR** connects to the [OXR parsing service](https://github.com/SoMarkAI/OXR#14-send-a-parsing-request) at `http://127.0.0.1:8000/v1` by default and falls back to HAPZO’s PDF text and OCR reader when the service is unavailable. Set `HAPZO_OXR_URL` to a different loopback base URL before launch if needed. **Hosted SoMark OXR** sends PDF and image uploads to SoMark’s free OXR API. **Local PDF text and OCR** uses HAPZO’s reader on this PC.
- Set `HAPZO_GEMINI_API_KEY` in the `.env` beside `server.mjs` and restart HAPZO to use Gemini for buyer PO import. HAPZO also accepts `GEMINI_API_KEY` from `.env` or the equivalent startup environment variables. Set `HAPZO_OPENAI_API_KEY` in the startup environment to use OpenAI when Gemini is unconfigured. The default models are `gemini-3.8-flash` and `gpt-5.4-mini`. Set `HAPZO_GEMINI_MODEL` or `HAPZO_OPENAI_MODEL` in the startup environment to change the corresponding model.
- With Gemini connected, an upload in **Buyer orders → Create buyer PO** opens the editable import review. **Garment tech packs → Import buyer PO with AI** can also read a saved PO with its supporting files. HAPZO gives Gemini the original and the available OXR layout. The review includes issuer and client order numbers, article references, colour and size quantities, buyer details and technical specifications. Check the quantities against the printed total before saving.
- In **Garment tech packs → Import buyer PO with AI**, select one buyer PO and any technical sheets or annexes. Use PDF, XLSX, PNG or JPEG up to 10 MB per file and 50 MB combined. **Read files with Gemini** sends the files to the Gemini API. HAPZO links each source file to the imported draft.
- In **Fabric specs → Upload tech packs**, choose one or more PDF, XLSX, PNG or JPEG files up to 10 MB each. **Read fabric specifications** shows any file errors and lets you choose a component by filename. **Load into calculator** fills the available construction, GSM and tolerance fields. Edit the inputs to recalculate the results.
- In the buyer PO and fabric upload forms, leave **Gemini model** on **Automatic** or choose a specific model. Statuses reflect this workspace’s reads over the past 15 minutes. HAPZO retries once with another Gemini model when the first model times out or returns a retryable error and shows the switch in the form.
- Confirm the buyer, PO number, style, colour, size, pieces and delivery date. Select a route and deliverable for any new lines. **Save PO and tech pack drafts** opens the first selected size. You can correct the prefilled fields and choose **Keep as editable draft** to return later. A separate save or revision release records the reviewed specification. Supply details absent from the PO, such as tolerances or marker data, in the draft or in a buyer technical annex.
- Open **Garment tech packs** and choose a buyer PO line and size. Record each fabric separately with its construction, fibre blend, yarn setup, shade, finishing, full and usable width, pattern or CAD marker and buyer limits. Add garment measurement points, seam instructions, thread, labels, packaging and performance tests as applicable.
- Use **Preview calculations** to check fabric metres and kg, cut quantities, garment grading, stitches per inch, trim requirements and carton counts before saving. Tubular widths are measured on one laid-flat face. Use a marker with the correct fabric width and garment size. Record actual cut-pattern areas with seam allowances or use the finished-pattern area and enter both directions of shrinkage.
- Use **Use calculated quantity for this stock item in PO planning** for fabric or trims you want included in BOM demand. The calculated quantity supplies the requirement for that stock item. Other BOM items come from the PO line. Only approved PO lines feed planning.
- Saving a tech pack on an approved line records a specification revision. Linked work orders and material requisitions carry their approved revision for inspection. In **Quality & lab**, select the lot and enter measurements against the applicable tech pack. The sample company has a populated tech pack on PO **DC-26018**.

## Garment production and the Decaste trial

- **Cutting** approves a marker by PO line and fabric component. Open a lay with the released roll lots and shade group, record actual plies and roll usage, and post the numbered bundles. Each bundle shows its contributing rolls and panel allocation. Return unused fabric to stock under its original or a new remnant ID.
- **Embroidery** approves the artwork revision and design file. Issue the original lots in-house or on an outward challan to the approved processor site. Record the thread and backing lots, run, and actual receipts. Each receipt records the custody balance at posting. Finance can link the bill to the outward challan and actual inward return.
- **Stitching** and **Garment finishing** use approved operation plans. Issue actual component lots and supplies, track work and transfers by line, and post the garment lot for Quality. For a rework lot, retain the original inspection and record the new inspection before packing. Operator output is recorded with the batch. Payroll posting requires an employee and payroll system.
- **Packing** applies a buyer PO assortment and records the actual garment lots, carton, pallet, weights, and warehouse. **Dispatch & logistics** shows each carton’s packing list and shipment. **Invoices & costs** and **Accounts & vouchers** post the corresponding invoice, tax, and inventory valuation.
- **Scan & trace** accepts lot IDs or lot QR images. A carton shows its contents and shipment, and an input lot shows affected shipments. Use **Digital Product Passports** to approve the product and market profile, assess the 49 mapping points, and review evidence before publication. A new draft version keeps the same identifier as the published version for that lot.
- In **HAPZO Decaste**, PO **DC-E2E-01** is a synthetic mixed-route order. Scan **DC-E2E-01-CARTON-A** and **DC-E2E-01-CARTON-B** to see each 150-piece shipment. Scan **DC-E2E-01-RAW-WARP** to see both shipments. Embroidery batch **DC-E2E-01-EMB-BATCH** retains the 200-piece first receipt and its 100-piece processor balance. The trial has a finished fabric draft and a garment draft.

## Scope for live operation

Configure approved certificate rules for your company and verify evidence with the issuer or tracker before posting claimed goods. Local extraction supports embedded PDF text and English OCR. GST and customs amounts use verified source documents entered by staff. Direct issuer checks, GST filing, bank feeds, e-invoicing and live PLC or MQTT connections require external service or equipment configuration. The DPP supports field applicability and scoped electricity, water and recycled content calculations. Product footprint studies, assigned GS1 identifiers and profile-specific approvals or signatures need their own source evidence.
